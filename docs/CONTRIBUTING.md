# Contributing to Low-Level Basic (LLB)

Thank you for checking out Low-Level Basic! The LLBC compiler is licensed under the **Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL 2.0)**. We welcome community improvements while ensuring creators maintain ownership of their own compiled applications.

### 🚀 Our Shared Ecosystem Goals

1. **Compiler Modifications:** If you make changes, optimizations, or forks of the LLBC compiler source files and distribute them, the MPL 2.0 requires that your modifications remain open-source under the same license.
2. **Your Applications are Yours:** Any software, application, or binary you build/compile *using* the LLB language belongs entirely to you. You are 100% free to use any license you want for your apps, including proprietary, commercial, or closed-source licenses.

### 🤝 Sharing Your Improvements (Encouraged!)

While the license legally requires you to keep compiler forks open-source, we want to build a collaborative community. If you improve the compiler, add a feature, or fix a bug:

* **Submit a Pull Request:** We would love to merge your optimizations directly back into the main `karunci/LLB` repository!
* **Give Us a Heads-Up:** If you choose not to submit a full PR, that is completely okay! We gently ask that you just open a GitHub Issue or drop a comment letting us know what you improved. This helps the whole community stay aware of cool new variants of LLBC.
